Any thoughts I should know?

You can contact me Plüschgiraffe and others interested in this site by joining the official discord server. There you can give me feedback, share your builds with others, report a bug or issue, or just say hi. I am happy for everybody who joins! If you want to report a bug, please first see below.

How to report a bug

In order for a bug report to be effective please include the following information:

  • Your operating system and browser.
  • What you tried to achieve.
  • How you triggered the bug.
  • If it occurred repeatedly or not.

Also, please make sure to report a bug only in the according channel on the discord server.
